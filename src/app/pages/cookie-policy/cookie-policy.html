II. WHAT ARE COOKIES?

Cookies are small text files that are downloaded to the user's device when they access certain websites. Their main purpose is to ensure the proper functioning of the website, remember user preferences and improve the browsing experience. Cookies do not contain specific personal information and are automatically deleted once their validity period expires.

III. COOKIES USED ON THIS WEBSITE

  • Duration: 30 minutes from its creation or last update.
  • Ownership: First-party (managed directly by the Website).
